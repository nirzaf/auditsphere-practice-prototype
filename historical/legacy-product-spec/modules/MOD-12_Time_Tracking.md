## How to demonstrate this module

These are source-derived rehearsal instructions. Exact final labels and pending controls must be checked against the current build; they are not a record of browser actions executed during this review. Where a required control remains pending, show its limitation or defer that step—do not simulate a successful business outcome.

1. Open Time Tracking and enter positive minutes against a permitted job/task.
2. Save Draft, submit and inspect the pending-review state.
3. Switch to an independent reviewer, return with a reason or approve.
4. Correct an approved record through a new revision rather than editing history.
5. Open related budget/invoice views and explain approved versus unapproved and already-consumed time.

## Expected client-visible outcome

Effective approved time drives totals; correction preserves the original approved rate and invoice history.

## Failure / denial / rework example

Self-approval, invalid minutes and a foreign job link are rejected; a correction must not become billable twice.
