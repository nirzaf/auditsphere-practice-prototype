## How to demonstrate this module

These are source-derived rehearsal instructions. Exact final labels and pending controls must be checked against the current build; they are not a record of browser actions executed during this review. Where a required control remains pending, show its limitation or defer that step—do not simulate a successful business outcome.

1. Open Team & Client Comms and choose an existing active client contact.
2. Compose from a permitted template and inspect resolved placeholders and attachment references.
3. Explicitly simulate Accepted, Failed or Unknown and inspect the local attempt reference.
4. Manually record a call/meeting or received-message note with visibility and optional job link.
5. Open the same communication from Client 360/job activity and compare its identity/history.
