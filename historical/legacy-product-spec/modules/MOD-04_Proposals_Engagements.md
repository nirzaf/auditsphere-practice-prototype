## Remaining work / demonstration limit

## How to demonstrate this module

These are source-derived rehearsal instructions. Exact final labels and pending controls must be checked against the current build; they are not a record of browser actions executed during this review. Where a required control remains pending, show its limitation or defer that step—do not simulate a successful business outcome.

1. Create a synthetic proposal from the selected opportunity/client; enter service, period, fee and terms.
2. Submit, switch to a different eligible person and review the exact revision.
3. Present it, switch to the permitted client management persona and record a manual response with evidence.
4. Return to staff, create the linked draft engagement, then obtain a separate professional acceptance decision.
5. Activate and review team/due dates; show suspend/resume or revise scope while retaining history.

## Expected client-visible outcome

Commercial acceptance, professional acceptance and engagement activation remain separate attributable decisions.

## Failure / denial / rework example

Attempt same-person review and an old presented-revision response; do not bypass either guard.
