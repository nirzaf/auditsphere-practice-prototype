## How to demonstrate this module

These are source-derived rehearsal instructions. Exact final labels and pending controls must be checked against the current build; they are not a record of browser actions executed during this review. Where a required control remains pending, show its limitation or defer that step—do not simulate a successful business outcome.

1. Open a job and add an internal note mentioning an eligible colleague.
2. Switch to that colleague and inspect the local notice and linked record.
3. Return as the author, edit the note and inspect attribution.
4. Use permitted moderation only with a reason; preserve prior history.
5. Switch to a client persona and show that internal notes, counts and references are absent.

## Expected client-visible outcome

A local notice is not an email or chat service; edits and moderation retain attribution.

## Failure / denial / rework example

A client or ungranted staff member must not appear as an internal mention recipient.
