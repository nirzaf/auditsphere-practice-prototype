# Project templates integrated into audit workflows

The prototype offers 33 original Office templates in their owning operational screens through the expandable **Working templates** panel. Search by account or document name, download the native file, adapt client/date/scope, and upload the completed copy through the existing engagement evidence workflow.

Source forms are working references. They are not automatically populated, issued, approved or shared. Saved proposal, acceptance, confirmation and release commands remain authoritative. Historical source names and dates must be adapted. The general external-audit engagement letter contains tax clauses outside the target scope and is explicitly flagged for adaptation. QFC engagement letters and resolutions are offered only when the selected client's jurisdiction is QFC or Qatar Financial Centre.

Raw historical source documents are available to staff inside existing authorized workflows; they are not surfaced on the client portal. Completed documents are shared through the existing scoped PBC/document workflow.

Excluded: Excel `~$` lock files, the unrelated loan-amortization schedule, and `WP-A1_Cash_and_Bank_Audit_Template.xlsx`, which contains text rather than a genuine workbook. The provided genuine cash-and-cash-equivalents workbook and cash-count form remain available. Originals have not been modified.
