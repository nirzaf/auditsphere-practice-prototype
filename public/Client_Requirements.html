    Random, Monetary Unit, Systematic Random and distinct Stratified Attribute methods; persisted reproducibility.

  2. Confirmations

    Critical confirmations block release; persisted Holding Letter never clears the blocker.

Findings expose balanced AJE authoring, independent technical review, management decision and current-TB reflection; SRM shows journal revisions, adjusted/unadjusted differences, SAD/PM comparison and significant estimates without double counting.

Flow 5 — Time to practice analytics

STE Audit Management Tool · Functional Requirements v2.1

  1. Daily time

    Record Engagement / FSLI hours with role charge-out rates.

  2. Exact profitability

    Total Engagement Cost = Σ(Logged Hours × Role Charge-Out Rate). Profitability = Contracted Audit Fee − Total Engagement Cost.

  3. Missing inputs

    Unknown rates produce Unknown profitability.

Daily time captures engagement, phase, hours, activity and effective charge rate; approval preserves the historical rate. Budget versus actual and utilization share staffing assumptions. Example fee 20,000 / WIP 25,000 gives 80% realization and 5,000 write-off; missing rate/cost/capacity shows Unknown. Scheduling changes must not silently reprice recorded WIP.
